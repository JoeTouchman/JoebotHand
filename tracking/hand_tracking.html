Curl = sum of joint bend angles from MediaPipe's 3D world landmarks (metres, centred on the hand), so it doesn't change with distance from the camera or where the hand is in frame. Fingers: MCP (flexion only; sideways spread removed) + PIP + DIP. Thumb: MCP + IP bend + how far its metacarpal swings across the palm (angle to the pinky→index knuckle line). Calibrate: hold the finger straight → "open = now", curl it fully → "curled = now".
